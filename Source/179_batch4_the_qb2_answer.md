## 3. The agent said WORSE on Mahomes. It was not. §0.2, again.

One agent reported Mahomes as a **live availability risk** — "not cleared for contact," "zero
preseason snaps," Justin Fields named as the Week 1 starter if he cannot go. That is a **severity
claim**, and §0.2 says a severity estimate must be measured, not reasoned. I checked it myself.

The Aug 25 source it drew on says the opposite of how it was summarised: Mahomes was **fully
cleared for practice at the start of training camp and has not missed a single practice or
scheduled first-team rep** since the Dec 15 surgery. The Sept 3 piece says he **"surged past all
expectations in his rehab, working with the first team throughout training camp."** Reid's
"day by day" is Reid's standard register, not a warning; Fields is simply the backup on the roster.

**Corrected read: on track, no formal declaration, no live contact reps — a DISCOUNT, not a
red flag.** This is the second time this session an adversarial read was directionally right and
one notch too loud, which is the failure mode §0.2 was written for. The agents were still worth
running: two of the three findings that change a pick came from them.
