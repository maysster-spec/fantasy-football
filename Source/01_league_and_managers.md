## 1. Full scoring rules

**Passing:** 0.2 pts per 5 yards (0.04/yd) · **TD pass 6** · INT −2 · 2pt conversion 2
**Rushing:** 0.1/yd · TD 6 · 2pt 2
**Receiving:** 0.1/yd · **0.5 per reception** · TD 6 · 2pt 2
**Kicking:** PAT 1 · FG 0–39 = 3 · 40–49 = 4 · 50+ = 5 · missed FG −1
**D/ST:** sack 1 · INT 2 · fumble recovery 2 · safety 4 · any TD 6 · blocked kick 2
**Points allowed:** 0 → +10 · 1–6 → +7 · 7–13 → +4 · 14–17 → +1 · 22–27 → −1 · 28–34 → −4 · 35–45 → −7 · 46+ → −10
**Fumbles lost:** −2 (all positions)

**Position limits:** QB max 3 · RB max 6 · WR max 6 · TE max 3 · D/ST max 3 · K max 3

## 2. Roster and format
12 teams · snake · 15 rounds · 60 sec/pick · roster 15 (9 starters, 6 bench, 3 IR separate)
Starters: 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 1 FLEX (RB/WR/TE), 1 D/ST, 1 K
Lineups lock individually at scheduled gametime · lineup protection OFF · no auto-reactivate

## 3. Waivers, trades, playoffs
Waivers: standard order, **resets weekly to inverse standings**, 2-day period, no acquisition limit, not FAAB
Trades: no limit, **no deadline**, 2-day review, 4 votes to veto — but **functionally dead, 3 league-wide per season**
Regular season 14 weeks · Playoffs 6 teams, weeks 15–17, single-week rounds, no reseeding · consolation ladder yes
Observes ESPN's Undroppable Players List

## 4. Keeper rules
1 per team · locks **one hour before draft** · designated round **End of Draft** (charged to round 15)
Must have been **drafted round 5+** the prior year and **rostered all season**
**Trade and free-agency acquisitions ineligible regardless of draft round**
Cannot keep the same player in consecutive years
Keepers active for 2026 and 2027

## 5. Payouts ($1,200)
1st **$525** · 2nd $225 · 3rd $150 · 4th $85 · 5th $25 · 6th $25 · weekly high score $10 · most points against $25
