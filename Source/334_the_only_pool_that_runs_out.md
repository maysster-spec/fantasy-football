## 3. HIS STRATEGY RULE IS RIGHT, AND IT BEATS WHAT I RECOMMENDED

Matt: *"early in the season it's better to gamble on other managers' backups over your own. This
gives that manager a more diverse portfolio."*

**The logic is correct and it is the standard portfolio argument.** Handcuffing your own starter is
negatively correlated insurance: it pays exactly when your season is already damaged, so the two
roughly cancel. Another manager's backup is positively correlated: his starter goes down, you gain
and he loses, and the swing is double.

**AND MATT'S OWN STATED OBJECTIVE SETTLES IT.** He said this week: *"I can't win the league on
8 to 10 points... not unless that player has the POTENTIAL to do more."* **Insurance is a floor.
He asked for a ceiling. I answered the floor question and he did not ask it.** Section 0.5(a2),
mine.
