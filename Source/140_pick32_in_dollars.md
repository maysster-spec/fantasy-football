## 6. What I could not check, and the limits

1. **Three things differ between my sim and the shipped engine at once** — objective
   (dollars vs lineup points), opponent model (§5 cheat-sheet vs ADP dispersion), and outcome
   model (position-conditioned draws vs projections). The QB separation appears in the sim's
   *points* column too, so it is not the payout table. It is also **not** the QB-conditioned
   pool's 0.926 form mean — both arms roster exactly one QB1, so a level shift on QBs cancels
   (doc 70's band logic). What carries it is **QB survival in the opponent model:** cheat-sheet
   opponents take higher-VBD RB/WRs first, so Hurts/Daniels/Burrow are on the board at 32 in
   94–99% of drafts and mostly still there at 41–56, which is what makes waiting cheap. §5's
   timing table says four real managers take a QB by round 2.5–3.2, so the real room may run
   QBs earlier than this model. **Bound, not a guess:** the most a QB run can cost the wait is
   the QB2→QB6 plateau, **11.8 points (§4.15)** — that would roughly halve the QB penalty at
   32 (−15 to −18 pts measured) but cannot erase the RB/TE opportunity cost. Burrow-at-32 stays
   behind under the worst case for waiting. `[DERIVED from §4.15 + this run; not re-simulated]`
2. **Continuation after 32 is static VBD under the QB2/TE1 shape, not the rollout.** Common to
   every arm, so it cancels in pairs; it is why the sim and the engine can disagree about
