## 2. THE GROWTH CURVE — HE IS RIGHT, AND IT IS ENTIRELY YEARS 1 TO 3

**POPULATION: WR seasons 2021–2024 with 4+ games who played 4+ games again the next year — 531
transitions. BASELINE: his own half-PPR points per game, weeks 1–14, in season N. OUTCOME: the
change to season N+1. Bootstrap CI, 4,000 draws.**

| transition | n | ppg N | ppg N+1 | change | 95% CI | target share N → N+1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **rookie → year 2** | 89 | 5.25 | 5.87 | **+0.62** | **[+0.11, +1.14]** | **16.4% → 18.1%** |
| **year 2 → year 3** | 84 | 6.51 | 6.66 | +0.15 | [−0.41, +0.73] | **20.1% → 21.4%** |
| year 3 → year 4 | 73 | 7.18 | 6.97 | −0.20 | [−0.97, +0.56] | 22.8% → 21.4% |
| year 4 → year 5 | 66 | 6.75 | 6.14 | −0.61 | [−1.44, +0.23] | 20.2% → 19.7% |
| year 5 → year 6 | 62 | 6.77 | 6.06 | −0.71 | [−1.59, +0.18] | 20.0% → 19.6% |
| year 6 → year 7 | 47 | 6.56 | 5.93 | −0.63 | [−1.52, +0.27] | 21.0% → 18.4% |

## 6. THE RECONCILIATION — BOTH THINGS ARE TRUE

**Growth and displacement are different events and one player can do either without the other.**
- **Jayden Reed and Shakir grew** — bigger share, startable, without passing an alpha.
- **Nacua displaced Kupp as a rookie** — and doc 245's list is mostly first-round rookies.
- **Pickens did both**, in year 3, taking 51% of Pittsburgh's targets.

**So the honest scorecard on Matt's receiver argument: the growth claim is CONFIRMED and the year-3
inflection is his exactly. The moving-teams half measures backwards on two independent objects but
is underpowered. And displacement — the thing I tested first — is a rookie-and-draft-capital event
(doc 245), not a development-curve event.** Three questions, three answers, and only the middle one
was ever what he asked.
