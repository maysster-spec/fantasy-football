**Reading:** the league is split. Four managers reliably take a QB inside round 4 (Snyder, Fleming, Kam, Ray). Four reliably wait past round 6 (Taylor, Lobsinger, allen, and Rychlicki trending late). Given 6-point passing TDs, the late group is likely leaving points on the table — but it also means **QB value reaches the middle rounds more often than the early runs suggest.**

Note that the two best managers in the league (Taylor, Lobsinger) are both in the *late* QB group. That is either evidence the late approach works here, or evidence they win despite it. Test against the actual computed PPG spread rather than assuming.
