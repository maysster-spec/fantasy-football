**The testable form, sent to Matt before any run:** a late pick (NFL round 4 or later, or undrafted) who produced

is unresolved.

## 2. R2c: does pedigree change what a producer keeps?

**POPULATION:** running backs 2015-2025 in section 1's events who produced (11.2+ half-PPR a game in relief); tier from
nflverse `draft_picks` by PFR id, late = round 4+ or undrafted. **OUTCOME:** share of the team's RB carries plus
targets in the first four games from the starter's return, minus his share before the absence.

| window | man who got the work: late / early | late minus early | designated backup: late / early | late minus early |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **all weeks (run first, the primary)** | +12.1 (39) / +11.6 (11) | **+0.6** [-12.4, +13.2], p=0.94 | +9.8 (24) / +11.7 (12) | **-1.9** [-13.8, +10.3], p=0.77 |

| starter back week 15 or later | +13.0 (10) / +5.0 (4) | +8.1 [-18.8, +34.0] | +22.0 (5) / +5.0 (4) | +17.1 [-9.5, +43.5] |
| window capped at week 14 | +10.7 (29) / +19.4 (7) | -8.7 [-20.3, +3.4], p=0.29 | +6.1 (19) / +19.2 (8) | -13.1 [-23.8, -1.9], p=0.054 |
| 2015-2020 only | 18 / 3 | -6.0 [-22.9, +8.6], p=0.59 | 13 / 4 | -10.8 [-24.2, +0.4], p=0.064 |
| 2021-2025 only | 21 / 8 | +2.8 [-14.5, +19.7], p=0.79 | 11 / 8 | +4.8 [-14.2, +23.6], p=0.66 |

**Verdict: UNRESOLVED, and the reason is a thin cell.** Over the full season pedigree does not change the share a
producer keeps; inside our season late picks keep 3 to 13 points less, against seven or eight early-pick events.
December leans the other way; teams out of contention giving their young backs the work is a plausible reason and is
not tested. Early picks who get a relief chance and produce are rare (11 in 11 seasons on the man who got the work),
and that cell limits every row. On the full season the smallest gap this could see at 80% power is about 18 to 19
points. The difference in differences (does production pay less for late picks) is -2.2 [-19.4, +13.6] and -6.1
[-20.9, +8.6]. Matt's doc 293 frame, *usage decides whether a late pick stays*, is not contradicted and not confirmed
by this.

## 3. What the cameo keeps

Backs who produced, 2015-2025, all weeks, the man who got the work:

| | n | share before | share after | out-used the returning starter in 2 of 4 | startable in the four games after | median half-PPR a game after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| late picks | 39 | 22% | 34% | 9 (23%) | 8 (21%) | 6.3 |
| rounds 1-3 | 11 | 28% | 39% | 4 (36%) | 5 (45%) | 9.1 |
| all | 50 | 23% | 35% | 13 (26%) | 13 (26%) | |

Startable is the RB bar, 9.92 half-PPR a game, over the after games he played (two or more). Late against early
p=0.13; with the starter back by week 14, 5 of 29 against 4 of 7 (p=0.050; medians 6.3 and 10.1); on the designated
backup, 6 of 24 against 5 of 12 (p=0.45).

**In plain terms: a back who produced while filling in is usually a committee piece afterward, about a third of the
work and startable about one time in four. For a deep roster that is a bench asset, not a lineup answer, and less often
an answer when he was a late pick.**

of cases and a returning late-pick starter in over half.

## 7. Limitations, named

- One event per team-season per position: the first absence, from week 5 on, of the weeks-1-4 leader. A second
  absence, and a leader hurt before week 5, are not in it.
- The guard drops backs acquired after week 4 and anyone who was not a genuine backup early. That removes stars
  returning from an early injury, which is its purpose, and also some real mid-season replacements.
- The after window includes the return game, when a starter may be eased back; it applies to both tiers alike.
- Relief scoring can rest on one game.
- Tier is the NFL draft round, a proxy for what a team invested; it misses second contracts, so a late pick who has
  since been paid reads as late.
- No clustering by player or team; a few players appear in more than one season.
- **Forking paths, stated rather than hidden:** two readings of the replacement, two windows, the in-season split and
  the starter's pedigree were all looked at. The all-weeks run on both readings came first; everything after it is
  labelled as a check or as suggestive.
- 2014 is in the weekly table and adds 9 and 7 events; 2013 snap counts exist and 2012's file is empty. Neither would
  settle section 2's thin cell.
