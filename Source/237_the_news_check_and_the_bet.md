## 3. "NEARLY UNOWNED", IN PLAIN WORDS

The percentage beside a free-agent name is **the share of all fantasy leagues everywhere that have
him on a roster.** It is not about our league. It is a crowd-interest reading, and it does two jobs:
- **2% owned** = almost nobody anywhere wants him, so he is certainly free here and nobody will file
  a competing claim. Matt wins **56%** of the claims nobody else files (doc 224).
- **20% owned** = a fifth of leagues have him, so somebody here probably wants him too. Matt wins
  **16%** of contested claims.
**Doc 236 §10 was one list: the best free backup running backs, ranked by how much the job pays if
the starter goes down.** Two things decide which to take — **how big the job is**, and **whether the
claim can actually be won.** That is all it was.
