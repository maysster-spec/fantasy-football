A player marked FA is added with the green plus: no claim, no priority spent. Do not ration what costs nothing.

Week one is the worst week to claim

Filling an empty slot is a different decision

When a slot is empty, almost any body beats zero.

From week 6, if a free kicker is 4–5 points a week ahead of yours, take him and keep him (+1.25 a week).
