## 3. THE RECOMMENDATION: DO NOT CLAIM HIM. WAIT FOR HENRY IN WEEK 5.

**Confidence: a judgement call, not a measurement.** It is close, and the deciding term is the priority, not the
players.

1. The claim is already booked for week 5, so pulling it forward buys **three weeks of a TE2 he would not start**
   over LaPorta anyway, at the cost of priority now.
2. **Henry is the better board player and nobody is bidding.** Waiting costs little precisely because his own_chg
   is flat.
3. Schultz's case rests on **two games** plus a projection correction whose size I have not computed. §4.35's
   ceiling on the best signal we hold is one spike week in ten.
4. §6's drought condition is not met, and LaPorta has missed nothing.

**What would change my mind, stated now so it is falsifiable:** LaPorta missing a game or landing on the report;
Henry's own_chg turning positive, meaning the quiet option is no longer quiet; or a recomputed VOR for Schultz on
corrected inputs that clears Henry's −18.3. **The third is mine to run and it is NOT YET RUN.**

**If a claim on him is already in, let it ride.** `waiver_report_2026.csv` has not been rebuilt since 19 Sept, so I
cannot see this week's claims, and nothing above is worth cancelling a placed claim over.
