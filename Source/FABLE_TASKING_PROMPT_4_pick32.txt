WHY THE ENGINE'S ANSWER IS NOT ENOUGH
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three reasons, and the third is the important one.

1. **Wrong objective.** The rollout maximises weeks-1-14 starting-lineup POINTS.
   SS0.3 says the objective is DOLLARS against a convex payout table ($525/$225/
   $150/$85/$25/$25), and SS0.3 states outright that the two "can disagree." Doc 70
   settled pick 8 in dollars for exactly this reason. Pick 32 has never been.

2. **A 0.15-point margin is inside every error bar the project owns.** SS4.1b puts
   honest RB/WR replacement uncertainty at ~+-1.5 VBD per player. SS4.23(a)'s
   positional-calibration HYPOTHESIS, if real, is a ~6% RB-vs-WR tilt. Either
   swamps 0.15.

3. **THE ENGINE IS BLIND IN A DIRECTION WE NOW KNOW THE SIZE OF (SS4.18c, doc 139).**
   `_lineup()` models exactly one kind of absence -- the bye week. Measured: a
   second QB on a different bye adds +2.49 to lineup value; a second QB on QB1's
   bye adds **+0.00**, because he never starts. Against SS4.17b's measured 2.98
   missed QB weeks and 5.54 missed top-2-RB slot-weeks a season, **the rollout
   prices roughly one week of absence out of three.** At pick 32 that is not a QB
   question, but it means the engine's ranking of RB-vs-WR-vs-TE there does not
   include availability at all -- and SS4.22(c) found prior-season availability IS
   predictive (players at <=12 games beat their projection by -16.2 points less,
   p=0.025; entirely a WR effect at -25.4, RB null).
