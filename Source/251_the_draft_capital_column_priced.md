## 1. THE TESTABLE FORM, STATED BEFORE THE RUN (§0.5(a2))

> **POPULATION:** doc 245's pair set — every team-season 2021–2024 where one receiver led his team
> with 60+ targets and was still on the roster the next year, paired with each teammate at ≤3 years'
> experience the following season.
> **RESTRICTION:** challengers in their FIRST NFL season.
> **OUTCOME:** the challenger out-targets the incumbent that season.
> **DIRECTION:** first-round rookies displace at a HIGHER rate than the base.

Doc 245 reported **who the displacers were** (9 of the 14 rookie displacers were first-round picks)
and never reported **how many first-round rookies there were**. That is a numerator without a
denominator, and it is not a rate. This run supplies the denominator.

## 2. THE RESULT

**Population reproduces doc 245:** teammates who played ≥1 game, n=364, 35 displacements,
**base rate 9.6%** (doc 245: 372 pairs, 9.9%). Same object.

| challenger | n | displaced | rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **first-round ROOKIE** | **15** | **9** | **60.0%** |
| everyone else | 349 | 26 | **7.4%** |

**Fisher two-sided p = 0.000001.** `[TESTED, n=364]`
On the looser population (every rostered teammate, n=794, base 4.4%): **9 of 16 = 56.2%**, p<0.000001.
**The effect does not depend on the population choice.**

By capital, first-year challengers only:

| challenger, year 1 | n | rate |
|---|---|---|
| NFL round 1 | 15 | **60.0%** |
| NFL rounds 2–3 | 30 | 3.3% |
| NFL round 4+ / undrafted | 293 | 1.4% |

**The cliff is at round 1, not at rounds 1–3.** §4.30's composite uses rounds 1–3 because it is
predicting a three-year conversion; this is a one-year event and it is a first-round event.

## 3. AND IT CONVERTS TO THE CURRENCY THAT MATTERS

Out-targeting a teammate is not a fantasy result. Rookie season, weeks 1–14, half-PPR per game,
against doc 12's measured WR replacement of **9.62**:

| | n | startable | mean ppg |
|---|---|---|---|
| **the 9 who displaced** | 9 | **6** | **9.77** |
| the 7 who did not | 7 | **0** | 6.18 |

**Six of the fifteen — 40% of all first-round rookie receivers landing behind a returning
60-target incumbent — were startable in year one.** Nabers 12.74 · McMillan 11.32 · Olave 11.23 ·
Egbuka 11.12 · G. Wilson 10.98 · Addison 10.88. `[TESTED]`

**AND THE MISSES ARE THINNER THAN THEY LOOK.** Of the seven who did not: **Pearsall lost 46–47 and
Golden lost 44–46** — a combined four targets from 11 of 15. **Jameson Williams arrived on a torn
ACL (2 games)** and **Travis Hunter played both ways (0 targets)**. Only Dotson, Smith-Njigba and
Odunze were beaten straight. This is not a reason to inflate the number — it is a reason not to
treat 60% as fragile.
