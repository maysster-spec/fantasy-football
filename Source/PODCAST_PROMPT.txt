PROMPT FOR NOTEBOOKLM / GEMINI — turn a fantasy podcast or article into the
three sentences you actually needed.
Paste this, then add the YouTube link, the audio, or the article.

You are summarising fantasy football analysis for one specific manager. Do not
give me general advice and do not pad. My league:

  12 teams · ESPN · 0.5 PPR · SIX-POINT passing TDs · start 1QB 2RB 2WR 1TE 1FLEX
  1D/ST 1K · keeper league, one keeper, must have been drafted round 5+ and held
  all season · I pick 8th, and my picks are
  8, 17, 32, 41, 56, 65, 80, 89, 104, 113, 128, 137, 152, 161.
  My keeper is George Pickens (WR, bye 14).

WHAT I WANT BACK, IN THIS ORDER, AND NOTHING ELSE:

1. PLAYERS NAMED — a table. One row per player they actually discuss.
   | player | pos | team | the call in ONE sentence | UP / DOWN / NEUTRAL vs his
   current ADP | where they'd draft him |
   Skip any player they only mention in passing.

2. THE THREE THAT MATTER TO ME. Of the players above, which are likely to be on
   the board at picks 32, 41, 89 or 104-137? Say why in one sentence each.

3. WHAT'S NEW. Only things I could not have known a week ago — a depth-chart
   change, an injury, a role, a camp report. If there is nothing new, say
   "nothing new" rather than restating their rankings.

4. WHERE THEY DISAGREE WITH THE MARKET, and their actual reason. Not "they like
   him" — the mechanism: target share, red-zone work, the guy ahead of him, a
   scheme change.

5. ANYTHING THAT WOULD CHANGE A DRAFT PICK. One line, or "nothing."

RULES:
- Quote a number only if they said it. Never estimate one for them.
- If they hedge, say they hedged. Do not convert a maybe into a take.
- No preamble, no "in this episode." Start at the table.
- 6-point passing TDs make quarterbacks worth more here than in the standard
  formats these shows assume, and 0.5 PPR makes pass-catching backs worth less
  than in full PPR. If a call depends on scoring, flag it.
- Keep the whole thing under one screen.
