## WHY THIS PROMPT IS NOT ABOUT OFFENSIVE ENVIRONMENT

I measured the environment idea before writing this. Across three season transitions
(n=221 WR/TE, n=113 RB), the change in a pass-catcher's target count breaks down as:

  team pass VOLUME .......  7.5% of the variance  (RB: 5.7%)
  his own target SHARE ... 93.4% of the variance  (RB: 95.9%)

Perfect foreknowledge of a team's pass-volume change is worth about +2 fantasy points
across a season. So do NOT research pace, no-huddle rate, 11-vs-12 personnel, or
"this coordinator throws more." That is the 7.5%, it is already inside the projections,
and it cannot move a draft pick.

Research the 93%: WHO WINS THE JOB.

## THE ASK

For each team listed below, a meaningful share of 2025 targets or touches belongs to
somebody no longer on the roster. For each one, tell me who absorbs it.

TEAMS, ranked by share of 2025 targets now vacated (my computation, from nflverse
2025 regular-season targets against the ESPN 2026 board, 08-30 pull):

  MIA 56%  ·  WAS 53%  ·  PIT 46%  ·  GB 38%  ·  NE 37%  ·  PHI 37%
  NO  36%  ·  NYG 35%  ·  ATL 32%  ·  KC 32%  ·  TEN 31%  ·  HOU 31%

For CONTRAST — these teams return essentially everyone, so an "opportunity" claim about
them needs an unusually specific reason: LA 0% · DEN 1% · CIN 7% · DAL 7% · SEA 9%.

## WHAT COUNTS AS AN ANSWER

For each open job, name the players competing for it and rank them. I need, per player:

1. WHAT THE JOB IS — slot, boundary X/Z, move TE, third-down back, goal-line back.
   Say which. "More involved" is not a job.
2. EVIDENCE FROM 2026 CAMP AND PRESEASON, dated — first-team reps, snap counts in
   preseason games, beat-writer depth-chart reporting, coach quotes about role.
   Training-camp hype without a snap count behind it is worth nothing to me; say so
   rather than padding.
3. WHO ELSE IS IN THE WAY, including anyone drafted or signed in the 2026 offseason.
4. YOUR CONFIDENCE that this player holds the job in Week 1: HIGH / MED / LOW.

## HARD RULES

- Every factual claim carries a source and a date. Undated = omit it.
- If you cannot find 2026 camp reporting on a job, write UNKNOWN. Do not reason from
  2025 usage and present it as news. An honest UNKNOWN is more useful than a guess.
- Do not tell me a player is undervalued, a sleeper, a breakout, or a buy. I have a
  projection and an ADP; I do not need your price opinion and I will not use it.
  Tell me who gets the snaps.
- Do not rank players across teams. One job at a time.
- Ignore kickers and defenses entirely.
