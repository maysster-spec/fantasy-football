# D/ST and KICKER — the two picks at the end

## PICK 152 — TAKE THE JAGUARS

Week 1 they play Cleveland at home. Best opening month of anything that reaches you.

| take, in this order | week 1 | first four weeks |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Jaguars** | vs CLE | **23.9** |
| 2. Bears | @ CAR | 23.2 |
| 3. Buccaneers | @ CIN | 23.0 |
| 4. Chargers | vs ARI | 22.0 |
| 5. Packers | @ MIN | 21.8 |
| **never** | | **Jets 14.1 · Bengals 15.3 · Commanders 15.7** |

Seattle and Pittsburgh are better and both go around pick 110 — they will not be there.

**Why the first four weeks and not just week 1:** across all 32 defences the best and worst
week 1 are only 3.6 points apart. Over four weeks they are 11.7 apart. One week is inside the
noise; a month is not. Drafting the month is also what keeps you off the wire in week 2.

**Why it matters at all:** who a defence PLAYS decides more of its week than who the defence IS
— 15.6% against 9.4%, measured on five seasons under our own scoring. The board's number is a
season projection with no schedule in it, so it cannot see this.

## PICK 161 — TAKE WHOEVER IS THERE

A kicker outscores a defence: **8.0 a week against 5.1, and a third of kicker weeks are double
digits.** So the position is worth having.

**But you cannot shop it.** Best and worst week 1 across all 32 kickers is **1.6 points**, and
**85% of a kicker week is randomness**. Whether the opponent is good barely registers.

**So: take the board's first name. If two look level, take the one on the better offence.**
Do not spend clock here.

---

*Both numbers are a tilt, not a forecast — about 0.8 points a week at defence and 0.4 at kicker.
Real, small, and free at picks you were spending anyway.*
