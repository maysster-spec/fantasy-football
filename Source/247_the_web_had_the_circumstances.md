things I did not have then:**
1. **Worthy is BELOW the 9.6 PPR pre-breakout benchmark** (8.93) while Franklin and Thomas are above it.
2. **His circumstance stack is the worst in his own band** — two established target-earners ahead of

**BOTH SIDES, AS THE RULE REQUIRES: the year-3 band rate is 15.6% and it applies to him. The
situational read says he is the weakest name in that band. The band rate is measured on 64 players;
the circumstance read is one analyst's paragraph.**

**THE RECOMMENDATION, ONE THING (§0.1f): HOLD WORTHY, AND ADD TROY FRANKLIN.**
Franklin is the only name that clears every filter at once — **year 3, 88 targets, 10.31 PPR ppg
which is ABOVE the pre-breakout benchmark, still on the team that drafted him (the 91.5% condition),

band rate is still his.
