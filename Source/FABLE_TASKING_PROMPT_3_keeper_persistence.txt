FABLE TASK #3 — DOES A KEPT PLAYER'S VALUE PERSIST?
Written 2026-09-02. Draft is Mon Sept 7, 8:00 PM ET.
This is the last unmeasured quantity in the directive that changes a pick.
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Read `00_PROJECT_DIRECTIVE.md` first — §0.2 (test before you claim), §0.3 (the
objective is DOLLARS against the §2 payout table), §1.1 (the ADP hard rule),
§3 (tag every claim), §4.18 (the finding this extends). Obey §0.1 in your reply.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE QUESTION, IN ONE SENTENCE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
§4.18 prices a late-round RB dart's keeper option at **≈ +8 expected 2027 points**
= 10.0% × ~+85 VBD. The 10.0% is measured (n=50 RBs, 4 transitions). **The +85 is
NOT.** It is the CURRENT board's VBD for an RB who finishes as position rank 5–10,
assumed to carry into the following season unchanged.

§4.18 says so itself, verbatim: *"Still unmeasured: whether next-year VBD persists
for a kept player."*

**Measure it.** If a kept player's VBD decays materially, the RB dart's option is
worth less than +8, and §4.18's draft-night rule at picks 104 and 113 — currently
"take the better player, because QB2's +11 and the RB's +8 nearly cancel" —
resolves toward the QB.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHY IT IS NOT ALREADY ANSWERED
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
§4.18's table measures how OFTEN a late pick becomes a keeper. It never asks what
he is WORTH when he does. Those are different questions and only the first was run.

The second is answerable from files this project already holds:
  • `draft_history_2021_2025.csv`      who was drafted where, and the Keeper flag
  • `code_adp_guard.load_preseason_adp(season)` for 2021–2025 — **the ONLY
    sanctioned market. §1.1 is a HARD REFUSAL: never use `espn_adp` from a
    historical pull.**
  • nflverse weekly logs, or the ESPN projection pulls for 2022 and 2024
    (2023's `raw_stats` are empty for 98 of 103 QBs — do not try to use it)

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT TO COMPUTE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Identify every player KEPT in 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 (the `Keeper` column).
2. For each, compute his position-relative value in the season he was kept, and
   in the season BEFORE. Use §2 scoring, weeks 1–14, against §4.1's replacement
   levels — or state clearly whichever baseline you use and why.
3. Report the RETENTION RATIO: value in the keeper season ÷ value in the prior
   season. Report it BY POSITION and with a confidence interval.
4. Then the number that matters: **what is the expected VBD of a kept RB in the
   season he is kept**, given he was a late-round hit the year before?
5. Re-price §4.18's option: 10.0% × (your measured value), and say whether the
   draft-night rule at 104/113 changes.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
GUARDS — these are not boilerplate, each one has already cost this project
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• **State BASELINE, POPULATION and SAMPLE SIZE for every number** (§3). A finding
  without all three is not portable and will be misapplied.
• **n is small and you must say so.** There are 12 keepers a season and four
  transitions. If a positional cut has fewer than ~30 observations the word is
  **UNDERPOWERED**, not "no effect."
• **Do not claim corroboration from a source you have just discredited.** Doc 92
  demolished doc 12's sample sizes in its §1 and then cited doc 12's output as
  independent agreement in its §2. Doc 93 rejected that and it was the only thing
  wrong with an otherwise sound document. Do not repeat it.
• **Survivorship.** A player is kept BECAUSE he was good. Comparing kept players
  to all players measures selection, not persistence. State how you handle it;
  the honest comparison is against players with similar prior-season value who
  were NOT kept, or against the same player's own projection.
• **Regression to the mean is the null hypothesis, not a finding.** A retention
  ratio below 1.0 is what you should EXPECT. The question is whether it is below
  the ratio for a comparable non-kept player.
• **§1.1 again, because it is the one rule that has bitten hardest:** historical
  `espn_adp` has drifted toward outcomes and is concentrated in exactly the
  players a backtest turns on. McCaffrey 2024 reads 16.0 against a true consensus
  of 1. Use the registry.
