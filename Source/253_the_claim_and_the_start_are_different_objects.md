## 1. THE OBJECT ERROR (§0.5(a2), one turn after writing the doc that forbids it)

**§4.31 measures whether a CLAIM BECOMES A SEASON ASSET** — the added player's points per game from
the add week onward reaching replacement. Week 1 scores 9.4% on that bar.

**Matt asked a START/SIT question**, and the comparison there is *a tight end versus an empty
lineup slot worth zero.* Nothing in doc 252 touches that comparison, and the answer to it is
obvious: **almost any startable body beats zero.** The 9.4% is the probability a week-1 add turns
into a season-long starter. It is not the probability he outscores nobody.

**THE SCOPE SENTENCE §4.31 IS MISSING, and it belongs in the directive:**
> **§4.31's week-1 penalty applies to SPECULATIVE claims — a bet on a depth chart, made to acquire a
> season asset. It does NOT apply to filling a hole in this week's starting lineup. Against an empty
> slot the alternative is zero and the claim is correct at any hit rate above nothing.**
