### [05:45] Fantasy Fallout: Josh Jacobs & MarShawn Lloyd
**Matt Harmon:** Josh Jacobs has been placed on the Commissioner's Exempt List indefinitely. Adam Schefter reported that Jacobs was charged with battery and criminal damage to property—both misdemeanors—by the Brown County, Wisconsin DA following an arrest in May. There is allegedly a video associated with the incident. From a fantasy perspective, Jacobs already carried a mountain of red flags (age, career touches, camp groin issue). You cannot click his name in drafts.

**Justin Boone:** I agree completely. With the exempt list, he cannot practice or play until further notice, and you cannot put exempt or suspended players in IR spots in most leagues. I have moved MarShawn Lloyd way up to RB26, drafting him in the sixth round. Lloyd spent the offseason at the Meyer Institute addressing his recurring soft-tissue issues, similar to Christian Watson's turnaround last year. He's 25 years old, highly explosive, and can catch passes. Green Bay kept Chris Brooks (pass protector) and traded for rookie Kaleb Johnson from Pittsburgh (dynasty stash), but Lloyd is the clear RB1 right now with top-15 upside.

**Matt Harmon:** I have Lloyd in the exact same spot: RB26, 69th overall. Green Bay will throw more, play more 11 personnel, and push the pace under Jordan Love.

### [14:45] Patriots Backfield: Rhamondre Stevenson (Riser) vs. TreVeyon Henderson
**Justin Boone:** Our first running back riser is Rhamondre Stevenson. Coming into camp, people wondered if TreVeyon Henderson would take over. But beat writers confirm this remains Stevenson's backfield. When New England faced tough games in the playoffs last year, Stevenson played 70.3% of snaps to Henderson's 31%. Stevenson is the trusted between-the-tackles runner and pass protector. Henderson brings explosive home-run ability, but Stevenson is the volume foundation. I have moved Stevenson ahead of Henderson.

**Matt Harmon:** Henderson's Yahoo ADP is 73rd and Stevenson is 76th; I have that flipped as well. Stevenson is an outstanding third/flex running back value.

---

### [18:55] Raiders Backfield: Ashton Jeanty (Faller) & Mike Washington Jr. (Riser)
**Justin Boone:** Ashton Jeanty is my first faller. He suffered a practice ankle sprain that had teammates taking a knee. While it's confirmed to be a low ankle sprain rather than high ankle, rookie Mike Washington Jr. has been sensational all preseason. Head coach Klint Kubiak shifted from calling Jeanty a pure bellcow in May to emphasizing a two-back committee. Jeanty fell from late round one down to the late second round in my rankings.

**Matt Harmon:** I still prefer Jeanty around the 1-2 turn over Breece Hall, but Mike Washington Jr. brings an explosive burst that ensures he'll command touches.

---

### [24:35] New No. 2 RBs Rising in the Ranks
**Justin Boone:** Backup running backs stepping into high-value handcuff roles:
- **Emmett Johnson (Chiefs):** Behind Kenneth Walker III, who has a history of nagging injuries. With Ameer Abdullah/Demarcado cut, Johnson is the direct beneficiary.
- **Kaelon Black (49ers):** The clear No. 2 behind Christian McCaffrey (who handled 450 touches last year and enters age 30).
- **Malik Davis (Cowboys):** Emerged as the primary backup to Javonte Williams after Jaydon Blue and Phil Mafah failed to secure the role.
- **Najee Harris (Giants):** Signed late and carved out early-down work behind Cam Skattebo as Tyrone Tracy Jr. struggled.
- **Seth McGowan (Colts):** Deep stash backing up Jonathan Taylor.

### [31:30] Titans Backfield: Tony Pollard (Faller) & Tyjae Spears
**Justin Boone:** Tony Pollard has plummeted down my board to RB86. Cam Ward has looked uncomfortable in Brian Daboll's offense, and the offensive line remains porous. Tyjae Spears played ahead of Pollard in preseason action, and rookie Nick Singleton could get late-season evaluation snaps. Pollard has a very low ceiling.

**Matt Harmon:** The entire Titans offense is a stay-away for me. Wan'Dale Robinson will catch short passes, but this team lacks touchdown upside.

---

### [36:25] Panthers Backfield: Jonathon Brooks (Riser)
**Justin Boone:** Jonathon Brooks has moved up to ADP 85. Chuba Hubbard is trending to play Week 1 after his hamstring strain, but Brooks has displayed high-end cutting and receiving ability in camp. Even if it opens as a 50/50 or 60/40 split in Hubbard's favor, Brooks offers the league-winning ceiling as the season progresses.

---

### [40:55] Broncos Backfield: RJ Harvey (Faller) & Jonah Coleman
**Justin Boone:** RJ Harvey has dropped nearly 10 spots in ADP. People realize rookie Jonah Coleman was brought in to inherit the early-down/short-yardage role if JK Dobbins misses time. Harvey is primarily a third-down pass-catcher; without a pure monopoly on volume, his standalone upside is capped.

---

### [44:35] Saints WR: Chris Olave (Riser)
**Justin Boone:** Chris Olave is moving into early Round 2 (25th overall). He finished as the WR9 in PPG last year and WR2 overall during the fantasy playoffs. With rookie Jordyn Tyson sidelined by a major hamstring injury until potentially midseason, Olave will command massive target volume in Kellen Moore's fast-paced offense.

---

### [47:55] Commanders WR: Terry McLaurin (Faller)
**Justin Boone:** Terry McLaurin is falling in my rankings. The arrival of Stefon Diggs was manageable, but Laremy Tunsil's injury has completely compromised the offensive line. Jayden Daniels may not have time for McLaurin's intermediate-to-deep routes to develop. I now rank Parker Washington, Christian Watson, DJ Moore, and Jameson Williams ahead of him.

---

### [53:20] Bills WR: DJ Moore (Riser)
**Justin Boone:** DJ Moore is rising up to 45th overall (ADP 56). He is the clear focal point for Josh Allen. Khalil Shakir and Keon Coleman have been banged up in camp, and Moore has proven 90-catch consistency in Joe Brady's offense.

---

### [57:40] 49ers WR: Mike Evans (Faller)
**Justin Boone:** Mike Evans has missed practice time all summer with soft-tissue issues (quad, groin, foot, hamstring). At age 33, entering a new offense with George Kittle, Christian McCaffrey, Deebo Samuel, and rookie De'Zhaun Stribling, his durability and target volume are major question marks.
