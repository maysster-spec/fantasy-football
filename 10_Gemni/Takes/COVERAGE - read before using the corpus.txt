TAKES_2021_2025.csv -- what is in it, and what is NOT
Built 17 Sept 2026 from the Gemini Notebook exports in this folder.

1,136 takes, 472 unique players, 46 analysts, 14 columns plus a `season` column.
De-duplicated on the full 14-column row.

COVERAGE, and this is the thing to state every time the corpus is used:

  season   rows   sources in the file   sources in the notebook   coverage
  2021      418            19                    65                 29%
  2022      197            53                    55                 96%
  2023      170            61                    62                 98%
  2024      107            47                    89                 53%
  2025      244            51                    51                100%
  TOTAL    1136           231                   322                 72%

2022, 2023 and 2025 are effectively complete. 2021 and 2024 are not, and any
per-season comparison that uses them is comparing different fractions of the
same kind of thing.

WHY 2021 IS THE WORST DESPITE NINE EXPORTS. Each 2021 export carried only 4 to 9
sources and the union across all nine is 19. Merging worked: it took 5 sources to
19 and 219 rows to 418. It could not reach the other 46, because the notebook never
processed them. That is a different problem from partial exports and it needs the
2021 notebook re-run with the current prompt, not more downloads.

THE LATER YEARS DO NOT NEED MERGING. One 2023 export already holds 61 of 62
sources and one 2025 export holds 51 of 51, so the multiple same-named exports
sitting in the notebook for those years are re-exports, not batches.

DUPLICATE IN THIS FOLDER: `ff_takes_2022_scored.csv` and
`ff_takes_2022_scored (1).csv` are byte-identical (sha256 90d543710b85). One is a
double download. Either can be deleted.
