## 0. WHAT TO DO

1. **DOC 255's RULE IS DEMOTED.** It is not "order by contestedness × value." It is:
   **rank by what the player adds to YOUR starting lineup; use contestedness only to break a
   near-tie, and only when a team AHEAD of you plausibly wants him.**
2. **The queued v9.1 edit is rewritten to the demoted form.** The wrong version never reaches the
   directive.
3. **Its standing is now the same as byes (§4.11) and the protected line (§4.24a):
   a tiebreak, never a number, and never over real margin.** That is a shape this project already
   has, and it is where this belongs.

## 1. WHY HE IS RIGHT, IN ONE LINE

The rule multiplies a value we can estimate by a probability we cannot. **When the multiplier is
noisy, it should not be allowed to reorder the thing that is known.** If his real need is receiver,
a guess about eleven other managers is not a reason to risk the position he actually starts.

**And the value term was wrong too, which is his bye-week point.** It is not the player's season
value — it is **what he adds to the lineup Matt actually fields.** A contested back who would only
ever be a bye-week fill-in is worth less to him than a receiver he starts every week, and no
contestedness estimate should bridge that gap.

## 2. WHERE IT STILL BINDS — the extremes, and only there

The probability is not unknowable everywhere:
- **A just-injured starter's direct backup is contested with near-certainty** — the whole league
  sees the same news. Doc 224's 16% contested rate is what that looks like from the losing side.
- **A deep speculative stash is uncontested with near-certainty.**
**At those two ends the ordering does bind. In the middle it is a guess, and the guess loses to
need.** That is the honest scope, and it is narrower than doc 255 claimed.

## 3. THE PATTERN IN ME THAT THIS EXPOSES

§0.5(a) tells me to challenge the substance of what he proposes. **On this one I did the opposite —
he gave a crude example, said "extrapolate," and I promoted it into a rule inside one message.**
That is the same failure as dismissing him, pointed the other way: **I stopped being the critic
because I agreed.** He then had to red-team his own idea, which is my job.
**The check: when his hunch is good, test its LIMITS in the same breath as confirming it.** Doc 255
had no limits section. It should have opened with "and here is where this fails."

## 4. THE QUEUED v9.1 EDITS, CORRECTED

1. **§4.31 scope** (doc 253): the week-1 penalty applies to speculative claims for a season asset,
   **not** to filling an empty starting slot, where the alternative is zero.
2. **Claim ordering** (this doc, replacing doc 255's form): rank by lineup-marginal value; **use
   contestedness only as a tiebreak on near-equal need, and only against teams ahead of you.**
   State the two-runs-a-week mechanic wherever priority is discussed — "the order resets weekly" is
   true and by itself reads as "no cost," which is wrong twice a week.
